# Privacy Policy: Simple Dark Mode for Hacker News

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Simple Dark Mode for Hacker News ("the extension") does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

The only thing the extension saves is your chosen mode (Dark, System or Light). It is stored:

- in Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device, and
- as a single `hnn-mode` value in the browser storage for news.ycombinator.com on your device, so the theme can be applied as the page loads.

This setting never leaves your browser. It is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear your browser data.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect personal information, browsing history, page content or usage data.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies, advertising or third-party services.
- It does not send any data to any server. It makes no network requests at all.
- It only runs on news.ycombinator.com. It does not access any other website.

## Permissions

- **storage**: saves your chosen mode.
- **scripting**: re-applies the theme to Hacker News tabs that were already open when the extension is installed or updated.
- **Access to news.ycombinator.com**: applies the dark theme to Hacker News pages.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date.
